# SCAD Video Encoder Benchmark

Experimental scripts accompanying the second SCAD research paper.

- Stage I: Screening of 37 pretrained video configurations.
- Stage II: OpenCV and NVDEC latency profiling of 17 configurations.
- Stage III: Five-seed evaluation of ten finalists.
- External Evaluation: DAD, CCD and ACCIDENT experiments.
- Analysis: Supporting experimental analysis.

These scripts are provided as research reference implementations and supporting experimental records, not as a standalone executable package.

Dataset: https://github.com/WhoCares258/SCAD
